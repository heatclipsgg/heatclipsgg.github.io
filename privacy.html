Privacy Policy

GTA Clipper · last updated 16 November 2026

GTA Clipper is a private command-line tool used only by the owner of the Heat Clips channel to upload videos to their own YouTube channel, Instagram account and Facebook page. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Google and YouTube data

When the owner signs in with Google, GTA Clipper requests access to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read the name of the owner's own channel (youtube.readonly), so the owner can confirm the right channel is connected. It does not read, store or share any other data from Google or YouTube.

GTA Clipper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. GTA Clipper uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Storage

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sent to anyone other than Google. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Viewer and streamer data

GTA Clipper does not collect data about viewers. It uses only public information about Twitch clips (title, streamer name, view count) to choose and credit clips.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer removes all stored access.

Contact

Send a direct message to @heatclipsgg on Instagram or YouTube.
