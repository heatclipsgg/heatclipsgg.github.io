Heat Clips

Short vertical clips from Twitch streamers.

Heat Clips shares the best moments from public Twitch clips as short vertical videos on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. Every video credits the streamer and links to their Twitch channel.

GTA Clipper

GTA Clipper is the private tool behind the channel. It runs only on the channel owner's own computer. It picks popular public Twitch clips, turns them into vertical videos with subtitles, and uploads them to the Heat Clips accounts. It has no other users and does not access any other account or channel.

Streamers

If you are a streamer and want your clips removed or not used, send a direct message to @heatclipsgg on Instagram or YouTube. We remove them and stop using your clips.
